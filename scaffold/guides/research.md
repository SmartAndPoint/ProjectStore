## Must

- Question is sharp — answerable by yes/no/comparison, not 'tell me about X'.
- TL;DR is a single paragraph that gives the conclusion before the body.
- Method describes how findings were gathered (docs read, benchmarks run, experts asked) — not just 'I researched'.
- Comparison table has populated Pros AND Cons cells; empty cells flagged.
- Conclusion explicitly ties back to the original Question. Recommendation is actionable.
- References are linkable (URL / file path), not 'see [some doc]'.
- No unstated benchmarks ('it's faster' without numbers).
- Findings are evidence-based, not opinion. Where opinion appears, it's labelled.
